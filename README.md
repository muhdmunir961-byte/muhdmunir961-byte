I'm an **automotive engineering student** who likes solving problems with software — from ESP32-connected dashboards to web platforms and everyday tools. I care about usable interfaces, thoughtful architecture, and building things people can actually use.

I spend most of my time exploring **full-stack development**, **connected devices**, and the space between **engineering and product design**.
